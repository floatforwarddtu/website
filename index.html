About us

From a research idea to the water in under a year

Float Forward DTU was initiated by a group of 10 professors with a passion for maritime engineering. Research had shown that a new linear fin propulsion system would be a strong alternative to conventional propellers, with a hydrodynamic efficiency of 90%.

A student team was formed with the mission of competing in the Monaco Energy Boat Challenge. Racing in the energy class gives us the opportunity to show what these technologies can do in the real world.

The linear fin propulsion system is one of many projects Float Forward DTU will undertake in the coming years, all launched around a common interest: engineering a greener future by converting to sustainable energy sources such as electricity and hydrogen.

In collaboration with the DTU Roadrunner team, we learn from each other while investigating the development of hydrogen engines and fuel cells. With almost 40 students and 10 professors, we finally have a team that can spark interest in the maritime industry.

Rookie of the Year 2025

Float Forward made its debut at the Monaco Energy Boat Challenge in July 2025, finishing 7th overall among 20 international teams. The highlight of the event was a 5th place in the Endurance Race, showcasing the team's engineering work and determination.

That first performance earned Float Forward the Rookie of the Year award, going beyond the goals set for the team's first season.
